# game/play

A match, played: `PlaySession` builds the level, steps the simulation once a physics tick with the player's command
and hands the tick's events on; everything else here draws the state every rendered frame, between the last two ticks
(docs/ARCHITECTURE.md › Contracts 1 and 3). Nothing here writes simulation state.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `play_session.gd` | `PlaySession`: the map, the simulation, the views, built in four steps (map, level, teams, views; one a frame while `staged`, reported by `progressed` for the loading screen, then `started` once drawn); you play `player_team` (Alpha or Beta); Esc and focus loss pause (the pause menu names the map, mode, round and seed); `left` back to the title; the smoke test's autopilot; a counted match's XP goes to the HUD (`Hud.show_xp`) when it is settled; `practice`: the range alone with its targets, no bots or rounds, the timed challenge (`set_challenge`, switched on the pause menu and saved as `Settings.practice_challenge`) keeping its best in Records; the HUD's readout and challenge display come from `Hud.setup_range`; the Loadout key (`loadout`) or the pause menu's row sends `loadout_requested`, `practice_spot` takes where you stood and `resume` puts you back there on the range built again |
| `practice_spot.gd` | `PracticeSpot`: where you stood on the range (place, view, stance, slot, holster, challenge switch, seed), taken when the Loadout opens and given back to the new range |
| `first_person_view.gd` | `FirstPersonView`: the camera at the leaned eye (`Lean.offset`), rolled a little in a lean and eased out of one a wall cuts short, dipped and tipped over a vault, turned by the mouse every frame, lifted by recoil, zoomed by the sight |
| `replica_view.gd` | `ReplicaView`: the replica in hand from its model: hip and sights, draw, the holster, sprint carry, reload cant and clip, kick, sway, pulled back from walls, a gas pistol's slide locked back on empty, the fire selector at its mode (`SelectorPose`), in the Kit's paint for its slot, with its parts; aimed along an optic's sight line |
| `replica_parts.gd`, `sight_fold.gd` | `ReplicaParts`: a replica's parts fitted from their models (`assets/models/replicaParts/`), the one way the hands, the figures and the menu pictures do it: the standard magazine or flash hider replaced, a longer barrel carrying the muzzle out, the iron sights folded under an optic (`SightFold`, held over the clips in hand) |
| `optic_glass.gd`, `optic_glass.gdshader` | `OpticGlass`: the red dot's and the scope's glass in hand, the view through it drawn by a second camera without your replica (the scope's magnified, only inside its glass), with the lit dot or the reticle; draws only while a sight is raised |
| `bb_view.gd`, `puff_view.gd` | BBs in the air and the puffs where they land, each one MultiMesh |
| `flag_view.gd`, `flag_cloth.gdshader` | `FlagView`: Attack / Defend's pole at the map's flag spot, visual only (no collider): the flag climbing the rope with the progress in the attackers' colour, waving in a shader, and the reach ring coloured by who works the rope (white idle, attackers raising, defenders lowering, yellow contested) |
| `range_targets_view.gd`, `range_view.gd` | Practice on the range: `RangeTargetsView` draws `GameState.targets` in greybox (RANGE_VISUALS): white plates on posts that swing back when hit, plywood figures the size of the hit volume falling back and rising, a mover's rails and trolley, a pop-up's motor box, a swinger's frame and arm, the challenge's lit glow and START plate; `RangeView` the floor lines and wall boards at each marked distance |
| `range_pilot.gd` | `RangePilot`: the smoke test's pilot in practice, shooting the nearest still plate |
| `figure_view.gd` | `FigureView`: another player as a greybox figure in its team colour (legs, and a torso, head and arm that tilt about the hip pivot in a lean), holding the replica it has out in its team's paint with its parts' figure copies, arm up and "HIT!" while calling a hit |
| `replica_paint.gd` | `ReplicaPaint`: a `ReplicaSchemes` paint put on a replica model's Body, Furniture, Detail, Accent and Metal surfaces (the Cyber Pistol keeps its own, plain grey with no glow under Realistic colours); one shared material set per paint, built once; the paint a bot wears by team |
| `selector_pose.gd` | `SelectorPose`: holds a replica model's fire selector where its Selector clip puts the fire mode, over whichever clip plays (a skeleton modifier) |
| `sim_audio.gd` | `SimAudio`: every sound from the events (a replica's by its sound profile: its power's, or its own, the Cyber Pistol's) (a vault's hands and kit too; on the range steel ringing, figures knocking, your target hits confirmed, the challenge's beeps), positional for others, plain for you (your own hit's tick on Interface, clear of the field's dip); others' only within reach of the ears the view is through (steps 22 m and 4 per 0.1 s, impacts 8 per 0.1 s, the range's targets 80 m, the rest 60 m); voices made once and shared through `VoicePool`s by priority; a cue never plays its last variant again; pitch and level spread by kind (none on interface cues); others' sounds and BB impacts muffled through walls by `SoundOcclusion`. Takes the level's `WorldQuery` and the `MapData` (for SP4) |
| `sound_occlusion.gd` | `SoundOcclusion`: muffling through walls from the listener's eyes (`SoundPath`'s knee and head rays to a character, one ray stopping 0.2 m short of a BB impact): open, or the half or full occlusion bus at the browser's level for the share (a silenced shot on the lower corner, at both levels); a share reused for 6 ticks, at most 24 rays a tick (over it, the last share; an impact open), none beyond 60 m where the share closes to full over 60 m more. On the smoke match: at most 5 rays a tick, 0.19 on average, 1.3 µs a tick (60 µs at most) |
| `voice_pool.gd` | `VoicePool`: katanashotgun's pool over the voices made at setup (a cue at its max reuses its oldest, else a free voice, else steals the oldest of the lowest priority no higher than its own, else drops and counts); `VoicePool.StartLimit`, the browser game's at most so many starts a window |
| `sound_mix.gd` | `SoundMix`: the Field bus muffled and turned down while you are out of play (until the match is over) and dipped under your hit and the referee's whistle (a round's or the match's end, the range's Time), shaped by a plain `SoundMix.DuckEnvelope` every frame; left at 0 dB with its filter off when it leaves play; sets the occlusion buses' corners and levels. Buses (`default_bus_layout.tres`): Field (Effects, with Footsteps, Power, Muffled (a silenced shot, low-passed), EffectsHalf and EffectsFull under it; Ambience) and Interface under Master; PowerHalf and PowerFull under Power; Master, Effects, Power, Ambience and Interface have a volume in Settings |
| `wind_dust.gd` | `WindDust`: specks of dust round the eye drifting with the match's breeze, one GPU particle box |
| `field_lighting.gd` | `FieldLighting`: the sun, the sky and the environment |
| `tick_pacer.gd` | `TickPacer`: how many fixed ticks each physics tick runs at the Dev settings' game speed (or the smoke test's speed-up), and the drawing fraction between them |
| `spectate.gd` | `Spectate`: the next bot in play Spectate any bot follows |
| `minimap_hearing.gd` | `MinimapHearing`: what your side heard for the minimap, fed each tick's events through the ears the view is through: one patch per enemy, the last footstep, vault or shot heard within its reach (a silencer halves a shot's), off their real place by a blur on the match's minimap stream (`Seeds.minimap_seed`) with them always inside it, renewed in place while they stay in it, fading after its life; a hit call removes theirs, a new round clears all; `beyond_view` for the rim. Plain data for the HUD (`PlaySession.minimap_hearing`) |
| `bot_intent_view.gd` | `BotIntentView`: the Dev settings' Bot intentions, a label over each bot saying what it is doing |
| `retro_pixels.gd`, `retro_pixels.gdshader` | `RetroPixels`: the Dev settings' Retro pixels, a full-screen filter under the HUD |
| `shader_prewarm.gd` | `ShaderPrewarm`: draws the late materials (a fading figure, a BB, a puff, the sign) as specks for the first frames, so nothing compiles mid-match |

You play the team the Match page picked (Alpha by default) with bot teammates against bots on the other. Tuning: `PlayTuning` (`game/config/play_tuning.tres`). Tests:
`tests/unit/test_play_session.gd`; sound `tests/unit/test_voice_pool.gd`,
`tests/unit/test_sound_mix.gd`, `tests/unit/test_sound_occlusion.gd`, `tests/unit/test_sound_path.gd`.
