### Two flags on Depot in Attack / Defend
- Depot's Attack / Defend has two flagpoles, A on A site and B on B site. The attackers win the round by raising their flag on either one.
- Each pole works on its own: attackers raise, defenders pull down, and both teams at one pole hold it still. The clock goes into overtime while attackers work either rope.
- A big A or B stands over each pole. The flag marker, the gauge and the warning ("Their flag is going up at B!") all follow the same pole: the one whose flag is going up (the highest, if both are), else the highest flag, else the nearer pole. The minimap shows both.
- Bots pick A or B each round. As soon as a flag starts going up at either pole, both teams' bots head there, even if the other flag stands higher: attackers to help it up, including when you start it, and defenders to pull it down.
