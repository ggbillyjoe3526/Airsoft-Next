### Two flags on Depot in Attack / Defend
- Depot's Attack / Defend has two flagpoles, A on A site and B on B site. The attackers win the round by raising their flag on either one.
- Each pole works on its own: attackers raise, defenders pull down, and both teams at one pole hold it still. The clock goes into overtime while attackers work either rope.
- A big A or B stands over each pole. The flag marker names the pole whose flag is highest, or the nearer one when neither is up. The gauge follows the highest flag, the warning says which pole ("Their flag is going up at B!"), and the minimap shows both.
- Bots pick A or B each round. As soon as a flag starts going up at either pole, both teams' bots head there: attackers to help it up, including when you start it, and defenders to pull it down.
