### Elimination's overtime flag (in development)
- When an Elimination round's clock runs out with players left on both sides, it now goes to overtime: a flag goes up near the middle of the map, and the first team with a player within a metre of its foot wins the round at once.
- Players of both teams at the flag hold each other off, and a team knocked out while it is up still loses. Untouched for 30 s, the round is a draw, played again as before.
- Depot's flag stands by the Box's east wall, Neon Heights' on Neon Avenue and Woodland's in the east meadow, each about as far from both ends' spawns. Attack / Defend and practice have none.
- Nothing shows or sounds when the flag goes up yet. Dev settings › End the round now ends an overtime as a draw, unless a team has a hand on the flag at that moment: then that team wins.
