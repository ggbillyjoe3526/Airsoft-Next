### Bots by night
- At night bots see only as far as the light lets them: about 40 m if you stand in the light of a fire or lantern, 25 m in the open under the moon, 15 m indoors and only 10 m under the trees. This counts on any map played at night, Neon Heights by night as well as Woodland (dev content).
- Bots on Normal and up keep out of the light pools when they hold a spot or hunt for you. Easy bots stand wherever their lane takes them, light or not.
