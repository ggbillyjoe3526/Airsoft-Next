### Robots in Looks
- Settings › Looks has a new Robots setting. Mixed is the default: half of each team are robots.
- Humans only draws everyone as a human.
- Robots change how figures look, not how they play. For now you only see them with Settings › Dev › Rigged figures on.
- The setting is saved with your other settings.
- Changing it in a match applies from the next match. A "From the next match." line shows until then.
- A rematch reads the setting again.
