### Rigged figures
- Settings › Dev has a new Rigged figures switch. It is off by default, so nothing changes until you turn it on.
- With it on, bots and other players are drawn as the rigged human and robot models instead of plain capsules. They stand still for now.
- Half of each team are robots. Team colours show on the human's plate carrier and on the robot's caps.
- Each human has one of four heads.
- Figures far away are drawn with less detail.
