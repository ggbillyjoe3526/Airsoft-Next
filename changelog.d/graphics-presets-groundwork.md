### Graphics presets to come
- Nothing looks different yet: Low, Medium, High and Ultra presets are now defined in one table, ready for the graphics settings to come.
- The game picks a starting preset for your graphics card: Medium for built-in graphics, High for a separate card, Low on the simple renderer, never Ultra.
- Your settings file has room for the coming graphics, window and frame-rate choices, and keeps everything you have already set.
