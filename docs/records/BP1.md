## What changed

1. **The walk stops by the toss's own line** (`game/sim/mag_share.gd`, `game/ai/bot_mag_share.gd`). The walk to a
   hand-off stopped at `mag_walk_up_to` (3 m) while the line from the giver's throw point (0.3 m under its eyes) to the
   catcher's chest was clear. The toss itself (`can_toss_to`) needs the line from the giver's eyes to be clear. So
   anything at eye height only (a window's top, a beam) stopped the giver where it never pressed. The new
   `MagShare.toss_line_clear(giver, receiver)` is that line. `can_toss_to` and `BotMagShare._up_to` both use it, so a
   walk stops at 3 m only where a toss can fly; otherwise it walks right up. The same applies to a bot walking up to
   you to wait for your toss. `BotMagShare._hit` is gone (it had no other use).
2. `tests/unit/test_mh1_qa.gd.uid` was generated by the import and is now committed.
3. `game/input/README.md` lists "Need a Mag H" after the order wheel.
4. **`ProcessCheck._first_arg`** (`game/core/process_check.gd`) decodes only the bytes before the first 0 byte. A NUL
   never reaches a String, and `char(0)` is gone. The result is unchanged.

## Decisions / defaults taken

- **One shared helper, `toss_line_clear`, not a second copy of the ray:** the walk and the toss can't drift apart again.
- **No wiki change:** Bots.md already says the bot tosses "once you are in reach and in sight", and the fix makes
  that true.
- **The NUL test counts the engine's own errors:** it recompiles `process_check.gd` with an `OS.add_logger` logger,
  because the result was already right and only the log was wrong.
