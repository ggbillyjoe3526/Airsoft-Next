## Goal

Woodland, the last task of step 26, is held by the match guards and measured by the balance report at 5v5 at night in
both modes and at every level, its frame cost is measured on every preset at 5v5 and 6v6, and PLAYTEST lists what the
owner tries before making it public. Three KNOWN_ISSUES rows left for WL10 are settled: the height guards' floor on a
terrain, the friendly-fire row deferring Neon Heights' night guards, and bots never hunting inside the hut.

## Approach

- `MatchGuard` (`tests/support/match_guard.gd`, new): the Elimination and Attack / Defend guard plays that
  `test_match_guards.gd` and `test_flag_guards.gd` each held, with their limits unchanged, now with a level and a time
  of day; every guard file reads it. A night-only map always plays at night.
- `MatchRunner.sized_for` gives the team size the game plays on a map (`MapCatalog.Entry.team_size_for`: Depot and Neon
  Heights 3 as before, Woodland 5). `lowest_floor` and `highest_top` take the terrain's `height_range` (KNOWN_ISSUES
  row, fixed).
- `tests/guards/test_woodland_guards.gd` (new): Elimination and Attack / Defend at Normal (16 seeds) and at Easy, Hard
  and Pro (8 each), at 5v5 at night; at Normal some rounds must be won at the pole, as on Neon Heights.
  `tests/guards/test_night_guards.gd` (new): Neon Heights at night at Normal, both modes.
- CI: a `night-guards` matrix of six jobs, Woodland's in five of 16 matches each (`tools/match_guards.sh` takes a test
  name's part as a second argument) and Neon Heights' night in the sixth. The day jobs are unchanged (PR #119's
  Elimination job took 18 of its 30 minutes).
- `balance_run.gd` plays Woodland too, at `sized_for`'s size; `tools/balance.sh`'s limit is 5 hours (Woodland adds
  about two hours single-threaded).
- The guards found bots knocking out teammates on Woodland (5 seeds over the first runs, below), so `BotCombat`'s
  friendly check changed at its root (KNOWN_ISSUES row, fixed): a teammate in play counts anywhere a run
  (`friendly_mate_speed`, 4.2 m/s) takes it during the BBs' flight; one faster than that (a sprint) also straight on
  and, for a bot, round its route's corners (`BotRoutes.point_ahead`, `BotWorld.bot_of`); and a miss reaches at least
  `friendly_carry` (60 m) from the shooter, as well as 25 m past the target.
- The gate then found a bot left standing on a low wall (`test_bot_vaults.gd`, seed 14; KNOWN_ISSUES row, fixed): a
  vault begun against the face was lifted onto the top by the mover's step, which `Vault.step` took for being held
  back, so it gave up there. A body lifted higher than the path is no longer held back (`test_vault.gd`).

## Risks

- Bots hold fire more often with a teammate near the line, most at long range (the run's reach grows with the flight:
  0.4 m at 10 m, about 6 m at 55 m). The guards' clock-out shares stayed at 0 to 3 % and every balance figure is in
  band, but how often a bot with a clear shot now waits is for the owner to feel (PLAYTEST).
- Woodland's guards take 12 to 14 minutes a job here with four at once; a slower CI runner has twice that before its
  30 minutes.

## Defaults taken

- **Both modes at every level for Woodland,** as the acceptance reads, not Attack / Defend at Normal only as for the
  other fields: night changes how Normal and up play (they keep dark), so each level is guarded there.
- **Neon Heights' night guards at Normal only, in a job of their own,** beside the day guards at every level: the day
  Elimination job is at 18 of its 30 minutes, and Woodland's guards already cover night play at every level.
- **A teammate anywhere a run takes it,** not a better guess of where it heads: each guess left another seed (a
  fighting teammate turning, one stepping off its route into a fight, one sprinting round a corner). The browser's check
  was straight on only.
- **A miss reaches 60 m at least:** a level BB from the eye lands 53 m out from the stock AEG, 50 from the cyber, 40
  from the pistol (`Ballistics.hop_up_reach`, factory dial), and farther down a slope; a teammate 33 m past a target
  23 m off was knocked out on Woodland. The browser's 25 m past the target alone stays for far targets.
- **The hut stays unhunted (KNOWN_ISSUES row kept):** standing a hunt spot on the highest floor a route reaches passed
  every Woodland guard but tripped Neon Heights' stay-home guard on the very seed WL6 saw (Pro, seed 7, by day): a Pro
  anchor bounding at a lane point 7.6 m from its spawn for 10.4 s of a 13.3 s round its team won, where the guard
  wants 8 m. Without the change the seed passes. The guard's limit is the owner's to judge, so the change waits.
- **The balance report plays Neon Heights by day, as before,** and Woodland at night, its only preset.
