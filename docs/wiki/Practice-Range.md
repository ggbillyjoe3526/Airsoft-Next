# Practice Range

The practice range is where you learn how BBs fly: no bots, no score and no XP. Pick **Range** on the Match screen
(its only mode is Practice) and press **START PRACTICE**.

## The targets

Three lanes of targets run out from the firing line, with hills, sheds, a water tower and a power line beyond them.

<!-- wiki:range-targets -->

There is no hit marker on the range: steel plates ring and fall, and figures fall, so you see every hit where it
lands. The **START** plate starts the timed challenge.

## The readout

After each shot a readout names your last BB's target and distance, where it landed, or that it flew out, with a tip
such as "Aim lower, or turn the hop-up down". Use it with the hop-up dial (4 and 5) to learn how far each replica stays
flat ([How to Play](How-to-Play#hop-up)).

## Timed challenge

Press **Esc** for the range's pause menu and switch **Timed challenge** on; the game remembers it between launches.

1. Shoot the **START** plate. The range counts 3-2-1, **GO!**
2. A bar shows your time and hits while you clear the targets.
3. **TIME!** shows your hits, shots and misses, and **NEW BEST** when you beat your best time.

Your best time is kept. A run with a Dev setting on says **NOT KEPT** instead.

## Changing your loadout

Press **L** (or pick Loadout in the pause menu) to open the [Loadout](Loadout) on the replica in your hand, with a
FROM THE RANGE note. Swap or tune your replicas, then press L again, Esc or **BACK TO PRACTICE**: you are back where
you stood, facing the same way, crouched or holstered as before, with your new setup. A timed run in progress is
dropped.

Leaving practice takes you back to the Match screen.
