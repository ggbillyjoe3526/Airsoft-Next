# Parts and Power

Parts and power sources change how a replica plays, never how hard a hit lands. Fit them on the **Customise** screen
of your [Loadout](Loadout); each part shows on the replica in your hands and in the menu pictures. Locked parts stay
listed, greyed out with the level that unlocks them ([Levels and Unlocks](Levels-and-Unlocks)).

The tables are read from the game's own files. How to read them:

- **×** multiplies a time or an amount: below 1 is quicker or smaller, above 1 slower or bigger.
- **%** adds that share on top: +10 % is a tenth more.
- **–** means no change.
- **Handling** is the time to bring the replica up after a switch and to raise a fitted optic.

A replica with no parts plays exactly as it comes, and bots on the other team carry parts by their
[kit](Bots#kits).

## Power sources

What drives the replica. A **battery** sets an electric replica's rate of fire; **gas** sets a gas replica's energy,
and a stronger gas kicks harder too. Energy still stops at the [site limit](Replicas#site-limits).

## Optics

An optic on the top rail. With one fitted the iron sights fold down and aiming looks through it. The red dot is a lit
dot on clear glass, with the view open round it. The 2x scope magnifies only inside its glass: the view round it
stays as it is, with nothing dark round the edge.

<!-- wiki:parts optics -->

- **Zoom** is how much aiming narrows the view. **Raise time** is the time to bring it to your eye.

## Grips

A grip under the rail. **Shake** is how long the wobble of a sprint or a landing stays in your aim.

<!-- wiki:parts grips -->

A vertical grip is slower to bring up but steadier after a sprint; an angled grip is quicker but shakier.

## Lasers

<!-- wiki:parts lasers -->

The laser tightens the pistol's groups.

## Magazines

Each replica's standard magazine is how it comes; these are the alternatives. **Capacity** multiplies the BBs a
magazine holds and **Carried** adds magazines to the replica's own (you always carry at least one).

<!-- wiki:parts magazines -->

- **Rattles:** the loose BBs in a hi-cap rattle as you move, so bots close by hear you even walking.
- The hi-cap carries as many BBs in all in fewer, rattling changes; the low-cap carries fewer BBs in all for a quick
  change and draw. The pistol's extended magazine holds half as many again, for a slower draw.

## Barrels

<!-- wiki:parts barrels -->

The tight bore is the accuracy pick: tighter groups at no handling cost. The long barrel is the reach pick: the most
energy and a little tighter, but front-heavy. Both count toward the site limit.

## Muzzle parts

<!-- wiki:parts muzzle -->

**Heard from** is how far away its shots are heard, by bots, on the minimap and on the sound cues: ×0.5 is half as
far. Silenced shots also sound muffled and quieter.
