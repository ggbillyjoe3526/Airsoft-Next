# Loadout

Open **Loadout** from the top bar. What you set here is what you carry into every match: your replicas, their parts,
power, BB weight, hop-up and colours. Every edit applies at once; there is no Save button.

## Saved loadouts

The **LOADOUT** tab keeps up to **five** named loadouts. Press **1** to **5** to pick one, and make a new one, rename,
duplicate, replace or remove it. The one picked is carried into the next match.

Each loadout has a **primary** and a **secondary** replica. The tab shows the two you carry and every replica you can
carry; replicas you haven't unlocked yet show greyed out with their level. A new player starts with the AEG Rifle and
the GBB Pistol. The Grenades slot waits for grenades, which come in a later update ([Roadmap](Roadmap)).

## The Performance sheet

Beside your replicas, the Performance sheet compares your setup with the replica as it comes: **better in green,
worse in red**. It covers energy, fire rate, magazines, handling and the rest, adds **Zoom aimed** and **Aim in** for
an optic and a **Rattles** warning for a hi-cap magazine, and says when the [site limit](Replicas#site-limits) capped
the energy.

## Customise

**CUSTOMISE** tunes one replica at a time:

- **Colour:** eight bold schemes, four plain ones, and a **Realistic colours** switch that shows bold schemes as plain
  colours.
- **BB weight**, from 0.20 g to 0.30 g, with a picture of the BB's flight, and the **hop-up** dial's starting
  setting.
- **Power source:** which battery or gas it runs on ([Parts and Power](Parts-and-Power#power-sources)).
- **Part tabs:** optic, grip, laser, barrel, muzzle part and magazine. A tab the replica can't take shows greyed out,
  with the mount it needs. Each part's card shows its picture and what it changes, better in green and worse in red.

The replica's picture shows the parts fitted, in your colours, drawn to the same scale as the others so a pistol looks
small beside a rifle.

## From the practice range

On the [Practice Range](Practice-Range), press **L** (or pick Loadout in the pause menu) to open the Loadout on the
replica in your hand. Change what you like, then press L again, Esc or **BACK TO PRACTICE** to go straight back to
where you stood.

## Bots' replicas

Your bot teammates carry the replicas as they come. The other team's bots carry kits picked by difficulty
([Bots](Bots#kits)).
