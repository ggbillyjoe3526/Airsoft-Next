# Roadmap

What is being built now and what comes after. **These are plans, not promises:** the order can change as the game is
played and tested. What has already arrived is in the [Patch Notes](Patch-Notes).

Airsoft is single player against bots, and stays that way: there is no multiplayer planned, ever.

## The road to 0.1

The game is built in **Dev** builds (0.1 Dev 1, Dev 2 and on), then **Beta** builds for balance, fixes and polish,
then **0.1**, which opens **Early Access**. Each build is posted on the
[Releases page](https://github.com/ggbillyjoe3526/Airsoft-Next/releases) with its patch notes. <!-- wiki:latest -->

### Built

Depot, the AEG Rifle, the GBB Pistol and the Cyber Pistol, every part and power source, the Loadout, levels and the
Armory, Easy, Normal and Hard bots with names, personalities and kits, Elimination, Attack / Defend and Custom matches, the
practice range in Depot's site, Practice on any field and the timed challenge, squad orders, the minimap, the hit feed,
scoreboard and match summary, the sound pass, graphics presets, accessibility options, the save file screen, the crash
screen and bug reports. The rest of this wiki describes all of it.

### In development

Being built now. Dev content is in the game but hidden: it shows only with **Settings › Dev › Dev content** on, until
it is playtested and made public.

- **Woodland**, a second field: a wide, rolling wood with a hill, a knoll and a ravine, played **at night** in 5v5 or
  6v6. Bushes hide you and stop BBs, fires and lanterns light pools in the dark, and bots see only as far as the light
  lets them.
- **Night play:** a low moon, stars and haze, with lamps and fires lighting the ground.
- **The weapon torch:** fit a torch to any replica for night fields. It lights the way and gives you away.
- **Glowing BBs:** green tracer BBs you can follow in the dark, flying exactly like white ones.
- **Practice on the dev fields:** Woodland's practice plays at night, with your torch fitted.

### Next

- **Elimination's overtime flag:** a time-out with nobody ahead puts a flag up near the middle, and the first team to
  touch it wins the round.
- **Pings** on the middle mouse button, and **asking a teammate for a magazine** when you run low.
- **Bot callouts:** your teammates call what they see and do in a callout feed, in words, never a voice.

### Then: the art pass

The greybox goes. Finished models for Depot and everything in it (the range too), human figures
with first-person arms and hands, and every replica, part and accessory, all made for the game and checked in play
under its own lighting.

## Early Access: the themed updates

After 0.1 the game grows through themed updates, each a small expansion built like 0.1, in Dev and Beta builds. The
order is still open: whichever comes first takes the next number.

### Replica Update

- A **spring sniper rifle**: one shot per bolt cycle, long reach and a loud crack.
- A **variable scope** and a **holographic sight**.
- **Grenades, smoke and flash bombs** for the Loadout's Grenades slot, airsoft style.
- **Chest rigs, belts and pouches** that decide what you carry, and an inventory.
- **Gas cooling:** rapid fire cools a gas replica's magazine and weakens its shots for a while.
- A chrono check before each match, more parts, and more replica types after the sniper (maybe DMRs, SMGs and
  shotguns; later gas blowback rifles and LMGs).

### Modes Update

- **Extraction**, solo against bots that hunt you down on the way out.
- **Medic:** a hit player goes down and calls for a medic before a bleed-out timer runs out.
- **Field rule presets** like real sites: CQB, Speedsoft and Milsim, and house rules such as pistols only.
- **Survival** (hold a building against growing waves) and **Rush** (Attack / Defend in stages), and maybe Team
  Deathmatch, Domination or Capture the Flag.

### Maps Update

- **New fields**, built for airsoft: an abandoned hospital, a trench line with forts, a quarry and more.
- **Neon Heights** goes public: a neon city block on three floors, with doors and furnished interiors.
- **Weather**: rain that masks footsteps, fog that shortens how far you see.
- **Prone**, with a field built for it.

### Progression Update

- **Challenges and badges** that pay XP.
- **A higher level cap**, with new replicas, parts and skins as rewards.
- **Replica skins and kit looks**: camouflage, plate carriers, helmets, goggles and patches. Looks only, never an
  advantage.

## 1.0

When the game is complete, Early Access ends and Airsoft is ready for everyone.
